WHO WE ARE

Trusted Logistics Partner Across India

SM RoadLines (SMR) is a family-owned transportation and logistics company established in 1989 with a single Tata 407 truck. Over the past three decades, we have grown into one of Odisha's most trusted and respected logistics companies, built on reliability, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

Today, SMR operates a modern transportation fleet alongside diversified businesses, including Logihub Warehousing (over 3 lakh sq. ft. of storage space), IOCL petrol pumps, and Tulsyan Automobiles, a Tata Motors Authorized Service Station. We have also expanded into food processing, construction materials, brick manufacturing, and wholesale distribution.

With decades of experience, a skilled team, and a strong commitment to safety and service excellence, SMR continues to deliver reliable, efficient, and scalable transportation and logistics solutions across India.
